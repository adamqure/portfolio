Snifr

A Wi-Fi e-nose for gut health tracking

Goal
Help people managing IBS, food sensitivities or elimination diets stop guessing about their gut health, with real answers they can bring to a doctor.
My contribution
Founding Engineer. I own the software that connects the e-nose to its users, from bringing the sensor online over Wi-Fi to the app and the AI insights built on its data.
How we got there
A Wi-Fi-connected electronic-nose sensor captures bowel readings and sends them to the app, which turns them into daily AI insights and a weekly FloraScore™ report with specific, science-backed tips, so users can see how diet and lifestyle changes play out over weeks and months.
Deliverables
A Wi-Fi-connected e-nose and companion app, daily AI insights and weekly FloraScore™ reports, now in beta.

Monovo

Securing mobile-to-device Bluetooth

Goal
Give patients a remote vitals-monitoring app that connects securely and reliably to at-home devices such as a blood pressure monitor, on both iOS and Android.
My contribution
Mobile Experience Architect. I built the iOS app from scratch, set mobile strategy across iOS and Android, co-invented the Bluetooth security framework with the hardware engineers, and later led the backend and web teams too.
How we got there
Designed security into the device link from the start: a multi-tier framework that layers protection on mobile-to-device communication. Established UI patterns, testing practices and engineering standards so both platforms stayed consistent as the product grew.
Deliverables
Production iOS app, cross-platform mobile architecture, patented multi-tier Bluetooth security framework (U.S. Patent No. 12,652,273), and end-to-end systems across mobile, backend and web.
